What "unclassified" does not mean. This page uses three cheap, mechanical signals: the disassembler's own instruction trace, the blocks declarations in annotations.json (script text and a handful of explicit byte/word tables), and two facts stated directly in this project's own docs (bank 7's graphics window, and the area-bank character-set/terrain accounting in AREAS.md). docs/COVERAGE.md is unambiguous that structurally nothing here is unresolved -- sprite storage and assembly, every song, all 76 rooms, every boss, the full script and more are all traced and, where the claim is about behaviour, checked against a running machine. Most of what this page marks red is territory those narrative docs already cover in prose (room streams, sprite tables, boss data, music) that just isn't captured by a simple parseable list the way script text is. Treat red as "not in this page's three mechanical buckets," not as a gap in the project.
