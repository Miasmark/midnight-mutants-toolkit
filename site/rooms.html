Midnight Mutants · Atari 7800 · 1990

Midnight Mutants Rooms

Every room in the game, drawn straight from the cartridge: the real terrain grid, each area’s own character set, and its own ten-band palette. Nothing here is a screenshot — these are rendered from ROM data by reproducing what the MARIA display processor does.

Plate width is information. A room is wider than the screen because it scrolls, and it is built from 20-character terrain slices. Each plate is drawn at true width, so a five-slice room is five times the plate of a one-slice room. Those same slices number the exit segments listed under each plate, which is how the game itself identifies a doorway. Dark areas are shown lit, as though the lantern were carried. Items are drawn at the coordinates the cartridge gives them, and each room names the creature it spawns — those link through to the bestiary. The diamond is drawn from MARIA palette 6, which the game rewrites from the random generator every frame, so in play it strobes; a single frame of that stands in here. Outlined squares are scripted terrain — a doorway, the well, a boss entrance. They mark the tile the player has to stand on, which is the lower part of a doorway rather than the whole of it.
