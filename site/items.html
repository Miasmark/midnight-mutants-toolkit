Diamond

id $08 · slot $1F45 · icon cell $F0 · palette 6

Nothing ever reads this slot — but the health it grants is read, and it decides whether you can be resurrected. f6:$520F adds 8 to hp_max at pickup; the death path revives you only if hp_max ≥ $30, which from a base of $27 takes two diamonds. Fewer than two and death is final. Drawn with MARIA palette 6, which the game randomises every frame, so it strobes — one frame of that is shown here.

Two icons have no colour of their own

The diamond and the plasmic pumpkin are drawn in MARIA palette 6, and f6:$4C70 rewrites all three of its registers from the random generator every frame:

JSR Random : STA P6C1 : EOR #$F0 : STA P6C2 : ADC #$10 : STA P6C3

The three colours are c, c ^ $F0 and (c ^ $F0) + $10 — hue-contrasted at matched luminance, which is why they strobe rather than flickering as noise. Both icons above cycle eight sampled frames; the cartridge changes them sixty times a second.

Pending items

Bit 7 of the header byte marks an item as pending: it is in the room but is not drawn and cannot be taken until the room has been cleared. DrawItemIcon returns early on BMI, so nothing appears at all.

Both strong weapons are pending. The knife and axe lie in the open; the blaster and mega blaster only appear once their room is emptied — which a knife cannot do, since it adds nothing to any damage accumulator.

The cross does eight things

Found by scanning all eight banks for every opcode that can reference $1F3E, plus the one selected-item test. Seven machine-code references and one check, and that is all of them — assembled piecemeal over several sessions before the exhaustive scan, each time as “another cross effect”.

Two slots are written and never read

The diamond and the mega blaster. Neither is a bug: the diamond's effect happens at pickup, and the weapons act through weapon_level rather than the counter. The counters exist so the icons can be drawn on Grampa's screen.

The other three weapons are read exactly once each, and only by Grampa's message selector. Carrying a weapon changes what he says; it does not change what the weapon does.

Grampa's advice is item-gated

A chain of tests at b0:$A3E2 picks his message: heart → knife → cross → axe → crypt key → blaster → lantern. The first that passes wins, so his dialogue tracks the inventory rather than boss_flags.
