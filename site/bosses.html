A note on the hitbox marks

Dr Evil's two boxes are drawn straight from the ROM values and land on his ears without adjustment, which is what confirms the coordinate mapping: the scroll origin subtracts boss_pos at b5:$B4B8 while the hit test adds it, so the two cancel and the box is fixed against the picture.

The Ram's and the Skull's marks are placed from play rather than derived — the top centre horn and the temple just behind the eye socket. Both portraits are narrower than the screen, so the window origin is not the blit column and the horizontal mapping does not follow from the ROM the way Dr Evil's does. In both cases the box size is the ROM's and the vertical extent is the ROM's; only the horizontal placement is observation.

The rules they share

One point a hit, and never from the knife. BossVsProjectiles is shared by all three. Its damage step reads weapon_level and skips the DEC boss_health when it is zero, so the axe, blaster and mega blaster are identical against a boss and the knife does nothing at all.

LDA weapon_level
BEQ skip          ; level 0 -- the knife -- does nothing
LDA boss_health
BEQ skip
DEC boss_health   ; exactly 1, whatever the weapon

They get faster the longer you take. A stage counter climbs one step roughly every 17 seconds and indexes a mask; the attack fires only when Random AND mask comes out zero, so the mask is the reciprocal of the rate. It starts at one shot in 64 and reaches $00 — every frame. Being hit advances the index by five while the flash is on, so striking a boss makes it retaliate harder.

All three move on the same sine table. dat_DD00 already drives the zombie head bob and the flying actors' drift; the bosses are its fourth consumer.

How a shot travels

BossActorMove (b5:$B9A4) moves every arena actor, and it has exactly one special case: a long velocity of $80 is not a speed at all, it is a marker. Those shots take their position from dat_DD00 — the same sine table as the boss's own movement — so they weave down the screen instead of falling straight.

The spread table both the Ram and Dr Evil draw from is dat5_BB38 = 80 80 80 80 00 00 01 FF. Four of its eight entries are $80, so about half of their shots weave and the rest fall straight, drift one unit right, or one unit left.

Downward movement is a table too: dat5_BB70[((flash & 1) << 4) + vel_cross], so the vertical step alternates between two values on successive frames rather than being constant. A shot is retired when its long position reaches $C8 or its cross position $BE.

Every one of these lives in the eight ordinary actor slots, and b5:$BB90 contact-tests all eight against the player — which is why the crawler, which is a creature rather than a projectile, hurts exactly like one.

Dr Evil alternates his weak spot

He is the only boss that moves his own hitbox. Bit 7 of $0045 picks one of two ten-unit windows 56 apart, and since the hit test uses exactly that box, only the currently exposed ear can be damaged. The counter ticks every other frame, so the side swaps about every 4.3 seconds. The same flag places his shot, so the projectile always leaves the ear you can currently hit — the tell and the threat are the same thing. The portrait above is what that mechanic is aiming at.

He also sends a wolf-like crawler along the floor: actor slot 7 with zero cross velocity, entering at the player's own height and travelling at half the speed of a shot. It is not a projectile, but the contact test walks all eight slots, so it hurts exactly like one.
